# Mission pivot: generic examples only, no employer-specific grounding

After finishing lesson 1 (which used the employer's real middleware and per-environment policies as worked
examples), Kawsarul asked to keep learning generic rather than tied to that specific project — and confirmed a
second time that no trace of it should remain anywhere in the workspace. `MISSION.md` and `NOTES.md` were updated to
drop the "ground examples in the real codebase's policies" constraint in favor of invented domains/policies shaped
like real-world ones; the reference cheat sheet's worked example, `RESOURCES.md`'s in-repo table, and lesson 1's
body text were all rewritten to remove every specific mention. Future lessons should invent a plausible
policy/domain rather than reference any specific employer's real infrastructure.

**Implications:** the sandbox app (`./sandbox/`) was already self-contained, not literal employer code, so it needed
only comment cleanup. Lesson 1 is otherwise left as historical record of what was taught, with all identifying
references replaced by generic equivalents so it still reads coherently on its own.
